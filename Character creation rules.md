Overall Character Creation Steps
Name and Description – Give your Chronicler a name and short description.

Choose Starting Licence – Pick one of four licences (Archivist, Diviner, Fixer, Guardian). Skills from your starting licence are easier to obtain, but you can learn skills from others later.

Assign Training – Each character starts with five “Bones”: a D2, two D4s and two D6s. Your licence defines a Strength and a Weakness. Put a D6 on the Strength ability and a D4 on the Weakness; assign the remaining D4 and D6 as you wish.

Choose Starting Skills – Select four skills from your licence’s skill path (detailed in the licence pages).

Choose Starting Interactions – Select three interactions from the options listed for your licence.

Licence Summaries
Archivist

Training: Strength in Observation, Weakness in Traversal.

Starting Skills: Two Observation skills, one Deduction skill, one Exploration skill.

Starting Interactions: Choose three from Diagnose, Sketch, Study, Take Samples, Talk.

Diviner

Training: Roll a D4 twice—first result is Strength, second is Weakness.

Starting Skills: Two Deduction skills, one Observation skill, one Exploration skill.

Starting Interactions: Choose three from Gift, Read, Sing, Soothe, Touch.

Fixer

Training: Strength in Deduction, Weakness in Traversal.

Starting Skills: Two Deduction skills, one Exploration skill, one Observation skill.

Starting Interactions: Choose three from Bait, Gift, Provoke, Read, Touch.

Guardian

Training: Strength in Traversal, Weakness in Deduction.

Starting Skills: Two Traversal skills, one Exploration skill, one Observation skill.

Starting Interactions: Choose three from Explore, Feed, Play, Protect, Provoke.
